Welcome to Dronacharya LMS, the official learning management portal of Officers Training College (OTC), AMC Centre and College, Lucknow. As the premier Category 'A' military medical training institution of the Indian Armed Forces, OTC imparts military, tactical, administrative, and clinical leadership training to Medical, Dental, Military Nursing Service (MNS), and AMC Non-Technical Officers across the Army, Navy, and Air Force. Institutional Heritage

Officers Training College — History & Heritage

Officers Training College, AMC Centre and College, Lucknow • Established 01 Nov 1957

Officers Training College (OTC) takes its origin from Officers Training Wing (OTW) that was raised on 01 Nov 1957 as part of the AMC Centre Lucknow. Another OTW was raised at AMC Centre (South), Secunderabad in 1962 after Chinese aggression, which was amalgamated into OTW at AMC Centre, Lucknow. The OTW was finally upgraded to Officers Training School (OTS) on 09 Aug 1969 and assigned the status of Category 'A' component of AMC Centre, which a was re-designated as AMC Centre and School. The OTS was assigned the responsibility of imparting military and administrative training to Medical, Dental, MNS Officers of the three services and also the AMC Non-Technical officers of the Army The nomenclature of this Category 'A' establishment from School to College was changed with effect from 16 Nov 2009. As part of cadre restructuring. the rank of Brig with appointment of Cdr OTC was upgraded to Maj Gen in 2008 & appointment re-designated as Comdt. It moved to its present location on Lucknow-Rae Bareilly Road in May 2015.
